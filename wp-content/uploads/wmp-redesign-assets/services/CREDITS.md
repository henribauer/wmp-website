# Services card images — credits

Used by the "Was wir können" / Kompetenzfelder flip cards (`#wmp-svc-root` in `index.html`).
All three are **wmp-ag.de own assets**: they sit in the wmp-ag.de WordPress media library,
uploaded 2024-07-24 and attached to the homepage (page ID 8, "Startseite"). wmp-ag.de had
originally taken them from Pixabay (the file names keep the Pixabay IDs), so the upstream
licence is the **Pixabay Content License** (https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/):
free for commercial use, attribution not required. Authors are listed below anyway.

Processing (same for all three): portrait crop 0.78:1 at full source height from the 1280 px
wmp-ag.de copy, resized to 900 x 1154 px (Lanczos, i.e. a 1.2–1.36x upscale) with a light
unsharp mask, JPEG q82 progressive, colour kept (the page applies `filter: grayscale(1)` in
CSS). Gentle gamma for set consistency: wirtschaft 1.00, medien 0.92 (lifted), politik 1.15
(darkened). Higher-resolution originals exist on Pixabay (links below) if sharper files are
ever needed.
